Before you start

Sign in, and learn the five places you will use.

  1. Open app.leadfinderos.com in Chrome or on your phone, and sign in with the email and password you were given. The first time, you will be asked to choose your own password.
  2. Your menu on the left has everything you need. On a phone it is the bar along the bottom.
Sales dashboardYour landing page. A quick view of where you are today.
OutreachYour own list of businesses. This is where you work every day.
InboxWhatsApp and text conversations with the businesses that have replied.
Find LeadsSearch for new businesses and add them to your list.
CoverageUnder More. Shows which towns you have already searched for each trade.

Good to know You only ever see your own businesses. Nobody else can see yours, and you cannot see theirs. Money and commission are not shown to salespeople.

1

Make a campaign for each niche

A campaign keeps one trade together, for example Plumbers. You only do this once per niche.

  1. Open Campaigns (the button at the top right of Find Leads and Outreach) and press New campaign.
  2. Type the niche, for example Plumbers.
  3. Choose how you will contact them: WhatsApp (you can send the approved opening message) or Call (you ring them, nothing is sent).
  4. Add a town if you like. The name fills itself in. Press Create campaign.

Good to know Every business you add later goes into the campaign you have selected, so make the campaign first.

2

Coverage: choose your towns

Coverage lists the towns for a trade and shows how far you have got in each.

  1. Open Coverage (under More). Choose the Trade at the top, for example Plumbers.
  2. The towns are listed biggest first. Tick the towns you want to search. Start at the top: bigger towns have more businesses.
  3. A bar appears: Find leads in these 3 towns. Press it.

Good to know Towns you have already worked show how many businesses you found and contacted, so you do not repeat yourself. You can search up to 10 towns at a time. For one town only, the Find leads button on its own row does the search straight away.

3

Find Leads: add the businesses

This is where you search and choose who goes onto your list.

  1. Find Leads opens with your trade and towns filled in. Check Adding to at the top right shows the right campaign.
  2. Press Find leads. When you come from several towns the search starts only when you press this button. It can take 20 to 30 seconds.
  3. The businesses appear. No Website (green) are your hottest leads.

Good to know You can also search by hand: choose One town, type a business type and a town, and press Find Leads.

3

Find Leads: choose and add

Tick the businesses you want, then add them.

  1. Some websites are run by an agency (a web company looks after the site). Use Filter, then untick Agency likely to hide them.
  2. Press Select this page to tick everything. Press Add to CRM. They now appear in Outreach, in your campaign.

Good to know Find Leads never adds the same business twice. If a business is already on someone else’s list it shows Added by … with that person’s name, and you cannot tick it. Select all also leaves out the sites an agency very likely runs; you can still tick those one by one.

4

Outreach: check your campaign

Outreach is your working list. Always check the campaign first.

  1. Open Outreach. Near the top, next to Campaign, pick the campaign you want to work, for example Plumbers.
  2. Only that campaign’s businesses are shown. Choose All campaigns to see everything.
  3. Tick the businesses you want to work (or press Select this page). A row of buttons appears. This is where you pick your route, on the next pages.

Good to know There are two ways to reach people. Use either, or both. Route A is quick and light. Route B is better for businesses you want to phone with something specific to say.

5A

Route A: message first, then answer replies

A short WhatsApp goes out first. You talk to the people who reply.

  1. On Outreach, press Select this page (or tick some rows). Press Queue WhatsApp.
  2. Choose the opener. Initial contact — original opener sends: “Hi, is this [business name]? Cheers”. Press Queue.
  3. The messages go out during the day (7am to 9:30pm UK time). Only businesses you have never contacted are queued.
  4. When someone replies, open Inbox. Their reply and the whole chat are there. If the AI has written a suggested reply, read it. Press Approve & send if it is right, Regenerate for another go, or Edit to change it.

Good to know You approve every message the AI writes. Nothing the AI drafts is sent until you press the button. If the AI has not written a draft, press Draft reply, or type your own.

5B

Route B: check the business, then call

The app researches a business for you, so you can open with something real.

  1. On Outreach, tick several businesses (up to 20 at a time). Press Check before calling.
  2. It asks ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI who they recommend, and it reads the business’s website to find problems. This takes a few minutes. You can keep working.
  3. The bar at the top shows progress: how many are ready, checking or waiting. You have 50 checks a day, and the bar shows how many are left.
  4. Each finished row shows scores like ChatGPT 1/3. Press Open next ready, or click any row, to open the prospect page.

Good to know A check never sends anything to the business and never changes their status. Results from the last two weeks are reused, so they do not use up your daily checks.

6

The prospect page

This is the screen you use on the call. Open it from a row, or with Open next ready. Scroll down: each section has a job.

HeaderName, phone, website, status and next action. Call dials from your phone. Log call records what happened. WhatsApp opens the chat.
Call in browserRings them from your computer. It is not recorded.
What we foundWho AI recommended instead of them, their scores, and the top website problems. Open with this: it is real and about them.
HistoryThe second tab: every call, message and change on this business.
Further down the same page
Call scriptWhat to say, step by step. Use your own words. There is one version for businesses with a website and one for those without.
QuestionsType their answers as you talk: the jobs they want, the areas, who decides, who looks after the website. It saves as you type.
What we offerThe offer in a few lines, so you can answer “what do I get?”
ObjectionsReady answers to what people say, such as “too expensive”.
ClosePick the plan and send the sign-up link (Step 7).

6

The prospect page: script and questions

Read the script before you dial. Fill in the questions while you talk.

  1. Open with the line from What we found.
  2. Ask the questions in the order shown.
  3. If they ask what they get, read What we offer.
  4. If they push back, open Objections.

Good to know Never promise a ranking. Say AI tools may find the business easier to recommend. The page reminds you of this.

6

After the call: log it

Every call gets an outcome and one next action.

  1. Press Log this call: the blue bar at the bottom of the prospect page, always there.
  2. Press what happened: Interested, Not interested, Didn’t answer, Call back, Wrong number, Left voicemail. More outcomes has Spoke to owner, Meeting booked and more.
  3. Set one next action with a date, for example Call back on Thursday (the Next line in the header). It then shows up in your day.
  4. Tap the star by the name for businesses that are genuinely interested.

Good to know Choosing Not interested asks you for the reason. Tell the truth: it helps everyone, and that business will not be contacted again.

7

Closing on the phone

When they say yes, the sign-up link does the rest: they read the agreement, sign and pay on their own phone.

  1. In Close, pick the plan with them. Findable Complete is the recommended one: £99 a month for 12 payments, or £999 once. Findable Essential is the cheaper one.
  2. Fill in the Questions above first (jobs, areas, who decides). The link will not send until they are answered.
  3. The plan terms and the money-back guarantee appear in plain words. Read them to the customer.
  4. Press WhatsApp, Text or Email to send the link, or Copy link instead. The app checks it is allowed first and asks you to confirm.

Good to know There is no submit button and no handover form. Everything you type saves on its own. When the customer pays, all of it goes to Paul automatically, and you will see Paid: your part is done.

What the customer sees

You never take payment yourself. The customer does it on their own phone.

  1. They open your link. Step 1: check the plan. Step 2: read the Client Service Agreement and sign it (name, role, a tick to say they can decide for the business).
  2. After they sign, the button Continue to secure payment unlocks. Step 3: they pay the first £99 on Stripe’s own secure page. You never see their card details.
  3. Paul then makes first contact with the customer within two working days.

Careful If a customer says they are still in a contract with their current web company, tell Paul. The agreement asks them directly, and while they are tied in we cannot take them on. Nothing is charged.

Daily routine: cheat sheet

Keep this page open on your phone.

  1. Inbox first.Answer anyone who replied. Read each AI draft, then approve, edit or reject it.
  2. Open Outreach and check the campaign.Pick the niche you are working today. See what is due.
  3. Top up your list.Running low? Coverage → pick towns → Find leads → Select → Add to CRM.
  4. Send openers.Select new businesses → Queue WhatsApp → choose the opener → Queue.
  5. Check, then call.Select up to 20 → Check before calling → Open next ready. Open with “What we found”.
  6. Log every call.Log this call → outcome → one next action with a date. Star the interested ones.
  7. Close.Pick the plan, answer the Questions, send the link by WhatsApp, Text or Email. Then move on.
  8. Finish the day.Clear every next action that is due today. Check the Inbox once more.

Good to know Stuck, or something looks wrong? Tell Paul. Do not work around a block: if the app will not let you contact someone, there is a reason.
