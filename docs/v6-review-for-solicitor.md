## What a lawyer should check

1. **Guarantee on Pay in full (5.4, 5.5).** A £499 refund is now promised on a measured outcome Findable does not control. Is the wording clear that the optional £29.99 hosting (a separate service) is not refunded?
2. **Transfer then reversal on Pay in full (4.3, 5.5, 8.4).** Copyright is assigned when the one payment clears, before the work exists, and 5.5 "reverses" it on a valid claim. Is that effective, or should Pay-in-full transfer wait until the Refund Window has closed?
3. **"Plan Period" for Pay in full (2.6, 9.2).** Six months from go-live: what if go-live is delayed by the client? Should there be a long-stop date?
4. **Minimum term wording for Pay in full (4.1, 4.2, 15.2).** Do the early-exit and termination clauses read sensibly where nothing further is owed?
5. **Consumer protection / unfair terms.** Clients confirm they act for a business (1.4). Check the pay-in-full refund limits (3.4) still hold up.
6. **Reminder promise (9A.5).** The agreement promises a reminder before the Plan Period ends; the app does not send it yet for the 6-month plan or Pay in full. Keep the promise (we will build it) or soften it.
7. **Electronic acceptance (1.2).** Unchanged from v5, but the signed record now includes the plan line. Confirm that is enough to evidence which plan was agreed.
8. **Version handling.** A client who signed v5 is never moved to v6 without a new signature.
