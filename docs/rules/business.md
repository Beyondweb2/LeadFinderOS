- ⛔ **Domain rule** (`domainAuthority.ts`, byte-identical in both repos): only build/connect the new site where the client confirms they own or control the domain; agency-MANAGED fine, agency-OWNED not (`domain_unresolved`). - **Commission** (`commission.ts`, numbers ONLY in `commissionConfig.ts`, `docs/commission-owed.md`, `docs/sales-page-monthly-commission.md`): ⛔ **The signed contract** (`fixed_v1`): first payment = `FIRST_PAYMENT_LADDER` by the sale's stamped number in the seller's month (one-payment plans: `ONE_PAYMENT_COMMISSION`), then `laterPaymentPounds` per later plan payment; hosting earns nothing. No figure typed on a screen. Collected by the window's last day: payable on `commission_payable_on`, the day AFTER the window (one DB trigger, NO backstop); after: the 1st of next month. A claim (`commission_claim_at`, or first payment gone in the window) cancels every unpaid line. A leaver is still paid. ⛔ `legacy`/`plan_v1` rows keep their numbers. Any refund or lost chargeback earns nothing, and after payout is an OFFSET. Mark as paid (`commissionOwed.ts`) only RECORDS a bank transfer. ⛔ Place and rate are STAMPED by the DB once (`stamp_monthly_commission_for`) — code READS them, never recomputes. Never key "who earns" on the sales role alone (Disable deletes the role; use `team_members.status`/`disabled_at` and `team_engagement_events`). `MONTHLY_TIERS` (TS) ≡ `monthly_tier_rate()` (SQL). A close's typed answers autosave (`autosaveQueue.ts`), one field, one write.

Order is structural (`onBaselineFrozen`; else `409 baseline_not_frozen`); pointers claimed by DB triggers, immutable; one replay per lead. 🔴 **A repeat's question cap comes from the STORED audit, never the caller.** Official baseline = Hook questions (locked) + Discovery = exactly 20 (`baselineRecommendation.ts`). Welcome Pack resolves from `baseline_audit_id` ONLY.
