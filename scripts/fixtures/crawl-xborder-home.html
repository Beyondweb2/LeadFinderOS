​
X Border Transport Training supplies Heavy Vehicle and Driver Training for anyone in Albury Wodonga and surrounds, including all areas of Riverina NSW and NE Victoria. ​We will teach you the fundamental skills required to operate heavy vehicles in a safe, efficient manner. 

​Being an innovative provider for Heavy Vehicle Competency Based Assessment (HVCBA),  we pride ourselves on delivering high standards of training to ensure all students understand the fundamentals of handling a heavy vehicle safely.

​​As a proud member of the Australian Driver Trainers Association, we follow the highest standards of safety, professionalism and driver education. Choosing an ADTA member ensures your lessons are with a qualified, trusted trainer.

We are a proud member of the National Road Transport Association (NatRoad), the largest road transport association in Australia representing thousands of members from owner operators to large national fleets.

​
Training and assessment will be delivered in accordance with the Transport for NSW Licencing process through competency-based training and assessment.
At the conclusion of the process, the successful applicant will be given a Transport for NSW Certificate of Competency which, when presented to the Transport for NSW, will result in the student being issued with their truck Licence.
