5. MEASUREMENT AND THE GUARANTEE
5.1  The "Access Date" is the date you have given us the access and information we reasonably need to start substantive work. We will record or confirm that date.
5.2  Baseline. On or shortly after the Access Date, before material changes are made, we run the approved baseline: currently 20 realistic customer-style questions, each asked 3 times on ChatGPT and Google AI / Gemini. We count how many answers name your business. Once approved, the questions are frozen for the guarantee comparison.
5.3  Re-measurement. We normally repeat the same measurement about four weeks after the Access Date, using the same frozen questions, the same scored engines and the same method. The date we issue the guarantee results is the "Results Date".
5.4  The guarantee. If the number of measured answers naming your business is not higher at the guarantee re-measurement than at baseline, you may claim a refund of your £99 initial payment by emailing us no later than 14 days after the Results Date (the "Refund Window"). Any increase, however small, means the guarantee improvement condition has been met.
5.5  If you make a valid claim, we refund the £99 initial payment, the agreement ends, and no further minimum-term payments are owed. Because the agreement ends before final payment, ownership of Our Work does not transfer under clause 8.
5.6  Payment Start Date. Your first recurring £99 payment is taken on the day after the Refund Window ends. No recurring £99 payment is taken while the Refund Window is still open. If the guarantee does not apply under clause 5.8 because required access or information was not provided, we may set the Payment Start Date to the day after six weeks from the initial payment and will tell you before charging.
5.7  The guarantee depends on you giving and keeping in place the access, information and approvals we reasonably need. If you cause delay after the Access Date, we may move the re-measurement and Results Date back by a similar period.
5.8  The guarantee does not apply if: (a) you do not give us the access and information reasonably needed to start within 30 days of the initial payment, or later withdraw access we reasonably need; (b) a website we build cannot lawfully or technically be connected to the agreed domain because of something outside our reasonable control; (c) a dispute with a previous developer, agency, domain owner or other third party stops or materially delays the work; or (d) information you gave us about ownership, authority, credentials, locations, services, access or your freedom to leave a current provider under clause 1.6 was materially wrong.
